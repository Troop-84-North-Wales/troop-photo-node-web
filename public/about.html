About Troop 84

Scouts BSA Troop 84 in North Wales, PA serves both boys and girls.

Our Troops

Troop 84 is a long-standing Scouts BSA unit in North Wales, Pennsylvania. We operate two separate troops — one for boys (Troop 84B) and one for girls (Troop 84G) — under the same charter.

Both troops are fully youth-led. Scouts plan and lead their own activities, from weekly meetings and campouts to high-adventure trips, while earning ranks, merit badges, and leadership skills that last a lifetime.

Scouts BSA is open to youth ages 11–18 (or 10 if they have completed the Arrow of Light or 5th grade).

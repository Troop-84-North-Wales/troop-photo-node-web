The calendar isn't connected yet

To show the troop's Google Calendar here, paste its calendar ID into js/config.js.

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Next to the troop calendar, click ⋮ → Settings and sharing.
  3. Under Access permissions, check Make available to public.
  4. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Calendar ID (looks like …@group.calendar.google.com).
  5. In js/config.js, replace PASTE_YOUR_CALENDAR_ID_HERE with that ID, save, and refresh this page.

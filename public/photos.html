Photo galleries coming soon

To show gallery buttons here, add share links in js/config.js:

  1. Facebook: open the album, copy the URL from the address bar (album should be set to Public), and replace PASTE_FACEBOOK_ALBUM_URL_HERE.
  2. Google Photos: open the album, click Share → Create link, copy it, and replace PASTE_GOOGLE_PHOTOS_ALBUM_URL_HERE.
  3. Save the file and refresh this page. Each gallery card appears once its link is set.

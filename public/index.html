Welcome to Troop 84

We now have two troops — one for boys (Troop 84B) and one for girls (Troop 84G). Both are active, youth-led Scouts BSA troops serving ages 11–18.

Who can join

Both troops welcome youth ages 11–18 (or 10 if they have completed Arrow of Light or 5th grade). New Scouts and families are always welcome to visit a meeting.
